# TaskFlow — Your Day, Organized

TaskFlow is a fast, focused to-do app that works seamlessly across mobile and desktop, keeping your tasks, reminders, and notes in perfect sync wherever you're working. Built for people who move quickly between planning and doing, TaskFlow strips away the clutter and puts your priorities front and center.

## Core Task Management

- **Quick add & delete** — Capture a task in seconds with a single tap or keystroke, and clear it just as fast when it's no longer needed.
- **Satisfying completion** — Check off finished items and watch them strike through, giving you a clear, motivating record of what you've accomplished.
- **Smart default sorting** — Tasks are automatically ordered by priority and completion status, so the most important, unfinished work always rises to the top.
- **Pin to top** — Lock your most critical items in place at the top of the screen, keeping them visible no matter how your list changes throughout the day.

## Plan Your Time

- **Weekday scheduling** — Assign tasks to specific weekdays in advance, so your week is mapped out before it even begins.
- **Multiple views** — Move fluidly between a focused Day view, a full Week view (weekends included), and a big-picture Month view.
- **Drag-and-drop rescheduling** — Change your mind? Just drag a task from one day to another — no menus, no friction.
- **Repeating tasks** — Mark any item as recurring with one-tap presets for Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly, so routines take care of themselves.

## Stay on Top of What Matters

- **Important item alerts** — Flag a task as important and TaskFlow will send a popup reminder a full day in advance, so nothing catches you off guard.
- **Notes on any task** — Attach a note to any item, indicated by a small icon right on the task so you know more detail is waiting.
- **Camera photos in notes** — Snap a photo directly into a task's note — perfect for receipts, whiteboards, packing lists, or anything easier to show than describe.

## Smart, Actionable Details

- **Tap-to-dial phone numbers** — Any phone number in a task or note becomes clickable, launching your phone's dialer instantly.
- **Tap-to-navigate addresses** — Any address is automatically recognized and opens directly in your device's local map app for one-tap directions.

## Works the Way You Do

Whether you're planning your week from a desktop at your desk or checking off errands from your phone on the go, TaskFlow keeps everything in sync — one list, every device, always up to date.
